About Shaheen Yousuf

Shaheen Yousuf is a Pahari poet and community figure from Kashmir. He has been associated with Pahari cultural and community activities and has worked for many years to support the development and advancement of Pahari youth.

Pahari Poetry

Shaheen Yousuf describes himself as a Pahari poet and is interested in preserving and promoting Pahari language, culture and identity through poetry and community engagement.

Work for Pahari Youth

A central focus of Shaheen Yousuf's public work is supporting Pahari youth, encouraging their progress and raising issues concerning justice and opportunities for young people.

Community Work

According to his own account, Shaheen Yousuf has spent many years working in connection with the Pahari tribal movement and community-related activities.

Personal Statement

“My aim is to help Pahari youth move forward and to work for justice and opportunities for them.”
